# Contributing

Constellation is being stabilized for a time-bounded hackathon submission.
Open an issue before proposing a material architecture or claim change.

Every contribution must:

- preserve the independent verifier boundary;
- keep all secrets and proprietary HexStellar implementation details out of the repository;
- add a counterexample-oriented test for new constraints;
- retain the exact certainty returned by external systems;
- avoid performance or optimality claims without committed evidence;
- pass `make lint`, `make test`, and `make verify-demo`.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under Apache-2.0.
